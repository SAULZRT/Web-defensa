# Design Brief: DarKlinca Defense Website

## Objective
Create a high-impact, professional, and aggressive website for "DarKlinca Defense", a personal defense brand. The site should inspire confidence, strength, and discipline.

## Target Audience
Individuals interested in self-defense, security professionals, and martial arts enthusiasts.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Vibe**: Aggressive, modern, urban, tough, elite.
- **Style**: High contrast, bold elements, large impactful imagery.

## Content Structure
1. **Header**: Logo "DarKlinca Defense" (text-based or simple icon), Navigation (Inicio, Clases, Sobre Nosotros, Contacto).
2. **Hero Section**:
   - Headline: "Domina el Arte de la Supervivencia"
   - Sub-headline: "Entrenamiento de élite en defensa personal para el mundo real."
   - CTA: "Empieza Hoy" (Red button).
3. **Services/Classes Section**:
   - 3 Cards: "Krav Maga", "Defensa Urbana", "Seguridad Personal".
   - Each card with a dark overlay and red accent.
4. **About Section**:
   - Philosophy: "No solo enseñamos a pelear, enseñamos a sobrevivir."
5. **Contact Section**:
   - Simple form with dark input fields and red submit button.
6. **Footer**: Social links, copyright, and brand name.

## Typography
- **Headers**: Bold sans-serif (e.g., Anton, Montserrat Bold, or similar available fonts).
- **Body**: Clean sans-serif (e.g., Roboto, Inter).

## Color Palette
- **Background**: #0a0a0a (Almost black).
- **Secondary**: #1a1a1a (Dark grey for sections/cards).
- **Accent**: #e60000 (Vibrant Red for CTAs and highlights).
- **Text**: #ffffff (White), #a0a0a0 (Light grey for secondary text).

## Image Needs
- High-quality hero image: Person in a defensive stance or tactical training environment (generate via implementation subagent).
- Textures: Subtle dark concrete or carbon fiber patterns.
- Icons: Tactical/shield icons.

## Technical Requirements
- **Format**: Pure HTML and CSS (Vanilla).
- **Responsiveness**: Mobile-first design.
